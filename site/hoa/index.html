HOA records

Find the association record. Then find the rule.

Texas maintains a centralized management-certificate registry. This page routes searches to the official registry and explains the regulatory distinction: TREC maintains the database but does not generally regulate HOAs.

Search an HOA

Important distinction

Database administrator ≠ HOA regulator.

The state registry helps locate management certificates. It does not mean TREC has general regulatory jurisdiction over HOA disputes.
