About

A new independent resource on a historic domain.

HomeownersOfTexas.org is independently operated. It is not the former Homeowners of Texas nonprofit/advocacy organization and does not claim continuity with that organization.

Mission

Organize Texas homeowner rights, regulators, public records and measurable market data into a resource that is easier to use and easier to cite than fragmented agency websites.

What this site is

A consumer-information and public-data research project. It emphasizes primary-source provenance, visible freshness, deterministic calculations and stable machine-readable endpoints.

What this site is not

It is not a law firm, government agency, HOA regulator, insurance agency, contractor marketplace or continuation of the former organization that previously used this domain.

Network data

Where relevant, property/environmental risk data may be attributed to Texas Home Intelligence as a separate data source. Cross-site citations should only appear when the referenced site supplies information materially relevant to the page.

Editorial standard

No manufactured authority.

Government attribution remains visible. Derived calculations are labeled. Missing data stays missing rather than being inferred.
